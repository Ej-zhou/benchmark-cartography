Effective concepts along each lineage

How many concepts does each benchmark really spread its activation over? Each item is one unit: its per-token mean activation vector, averaged over the benchmark's items. Effective concepts is exp(entropy) of each concept's share of that average, so 16,384 would mean perfectly even use. Breadth is the looser count of concepts with coverage χ ≥ e⁻⁵. The count grows with the number of items sampled, so the first chart is a rarefaction curve: effective concepts at 30 to 1,000 items and at the full size (mean of draws per point, base model). The bars below read every benchmark off that curve at a shared -item budget (mean of draws; whiskers are ± 1 sd). Hover a bar or curve for its numbers; click a bar to open that benchmark.
